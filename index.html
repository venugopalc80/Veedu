01 / The House

Welcome to VEEDU

Kerala, served differently.

VEEDU means home. It is a feeling as much as a place: the warmth of being welcomed, the aroma from the kitchen, and a table made for sharing.

Our cuisine draws from the coastal flavours of Malabar and reimagines them for a contemporary London audience without losing the soul of Kerala.
