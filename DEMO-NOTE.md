# VEEDU Website Concept

This repository is a **website concept/demo prepared by The Pixel Muses for VEEDU**.

It is not intended to present itself as VEEDU's current production website.

## Demo positioning

- Present the site as a proposed digital experience.
- Keep Menu and Reserve a Table as primary customer journeys.
- Do not invent confirmed dishes, prices, opening hours, telephone numbers, email addresses, reservation providers, capacity, testimonials or exact street address details.
- Clearly label conceptual photography and unconfirmed menu content.
- Do not use a CTA that suggests this concept is VEEDU's existing website, such as "Visit website".

## Proposed customer journeys

Home → Menu → Reserve a Table

Home → Our Story → Chef Jomon

Home → Gallery → Private Dining & Events

Home → Find Us / Contact

## Before production handoff

Replace concept photography and placeholder menu copy with VEEDU-approved assets and information. Connect the confirmed reservation provider and contact details only after VEEDU approves them.
