01 / The House

Kerala, felt at home.

VEEDU means home — a place of warmth, welcome, memory and food. This proposed digital experience gives that idea room to breathe.

VibrantEarthyEnticingDelectableUmami

The idea

A feeling,
not just a place.

From the aroma of a kitchen to a table made for sharing, VEEDU is built around the feeling of being welcomed home.

Inspired by Kerala and the Malabar coast, the concept creates a contemporary London setting without losing the sense of place, hospitality and regional character at its heart.

The VEEDU philosophy

Five letters.
One experience.

V

Colour, energy and the unmistakable spirit of Kerala.
