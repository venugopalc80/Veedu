Kerala, felt at home.

VEEDU means home — a place of warmth, welcome, memory and food. This concept page gives the restaurant a dedicated space to tell that story.

The idea

A feeling, not just a place.

From the aroma of a kitchen to a table made for sharing, VEEDU is built around the feeling of being welcomed home.
